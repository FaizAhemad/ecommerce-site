# Seller onboarding (MP-02)

Source implementation only; migration, deferred checks and production/device acceptance remain pending.

`/seller` requires login and shows the current account's application. Per owner override (2026-10-02), seller submission and admin approval do not require email verification until the owner asks to restore that gate. Applying does not set the account's emailVerified state; the app does not require or assign a special `seller@gadgify.com` address. The UUID-bound shop name, city, private business address, contact mobile, product description and GST declaration stay account-owned; address/contact are copied to private Shop columns after admin shop approval. GSTIN is format-checked only, not verified with GST Portal. If not GST-registered, the applicant must choose a reason; an enrolment ID can be supplied. This is a shop-level onboarding declaration, not product content. Approved sellers can update GST details through the versioned/audited `update_gst` action without changing shop approval; the shop's GST review resets to `PENDING` and external-shop purchase eligibility pauses until an admin reviews the new declaration. The form explains that a reason alone does not establish marketplace eligibility. Public shop/catalog DTOs never expose these private records. No arbitrary email, account ID, banking information or document upload is accepted. One application per account is the bounded initial workflow; multiple shops/staff management remain undecided. Repeating the same saved submission reconciles it; rejected applicants can revise using the current version.

`/admin/sellers` is administrator-only with paginated application review. Admin sees the private GST declaration and must explicitly choose GST eligibility review (`APPROVED`, `NEEDS_INFO`, or `REJECTED`) separately from the shop access decision. Granting shop approval requires `GST APPROVED`; a non-registration reason alone does not automatically qualify the seller. `NEEDS_INFO`/`REJECTED` GST status blocks external-shop purchase eligibility, even if the shop record itself remains approved. Product moderation remains a separate product-content decision. Decisions require a bounded reason shown to the applicant and the expected version. Pending applications can be approved/rejected; approved reviews can be edited, rejected or suspended; rejected decisions can be edited or corrected to approval; suspended shops can be edited, restored or rejected. Shop/membership changes, GST gate and the new review version/audit record share a serializable transaction. Rejection/suspension revokes membership; approval/restoration reactivates only the applicant's membership. No customer gains User.role ADMIN and no seller product/payout capability is enabled here.

POST seller/application and PATCH admin/sellers retain central CSRF and rate limiting. Seller writes use a dedicated quota; private client requests use session-generation cancellation. Application data is stored under reserved seller-application keys, excluded from generic Settings. Review audit keys retain actor, reason, decision and version. Ordinary seller reads expose only their own application; customer identity/contact data is not published publicly. Missing migrations/provider/database errors never imply approval.

Apply MP-01 ownership and seller-fulfillment migrations, then `20261002120000_marketplace_commercial_offers` (which adds the shop-level GST review gate) under MARKETPLACE_MIGRATION.md before approving or enabling external-shop purchases; regenerate Prisma after applying them. Existing shops receive `PENDING` GST review by default and remain unpurchasable until reviewed. No application/approval email is sent; application status is visible in the account and admins review `/admin/sellers`. Seller notification emails still require a genuinely verified recipient; this temporary application override does not bypass notification recipient checks. Tax eligibility, tax collection/reporting and invoicing remain for owner-qualified tax review. Retention, seller agreements and provider onboarding requirements remain owner decisions.

Focused seller application/catalog tests (18/18) and `npm run build:offline` pass, covering input limits, GSTIN format/reason branches, approved-seller versioned tax updates, unverified-account submission, duplicate submissions, stale review versions, membership binding, edited reviews, corrected decisions and shop-identifier collision protection. GST Portal status is not live-verified; legal eligibility and sales limits for unregistered shops remain subject to owner tax review. Database rollback/concurrency, authorization/CSRF, rejected resubmission, suspension/restoration, private cache switching, failed drafts, dialog keyboard/mobile states and production acceptance remain deferred.
