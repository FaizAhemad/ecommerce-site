# Seller catalog publication and purchase safeguards

2026-09-23 source implementation. This extends SELLER_WORKSPACE.md; it does not certify migration, deployment or marketplace payment readiness.

Admin content approval publishes a shop-namespaced Product, media references, approved ownership and a versioned commercial offer. The offer is either a fixed INR fee per unit sold (minor units) or a percentage of the discounted item price (basis points). A seller must accept that exact version before the product is orderable through Gadgify. Sellers can decline; admins can send a revised offer. Seller edits/archive withdraw the listing and clear accepted commercial terms in the same transaction. Product identity, historical references and stock reservations are preserved across reapproval.

Catalog/detail responses expose purchase availability but do not reveal shop names or slugs. Shop showcases link to published product details and show INR prices to authorized admins and approved sellers. Existing approved drafts require resubmission/reapproval to create their catalog projection; there is no automatic bulk publication.

Owner access update (2026-10-03): shop directories and shop showcases are restricted to administrators and signed-in users with an active membership in an approved non-platform shop. Customers shop through Gadgify's product catalog; public product images remain served for catalog listings, but the storefront no longer links to or labels the seller shop. The `/api/shops` metadata/directory responses and the page both enforce access; `/api/shops/access` exposes only a minimal authorization boolean.

Product list/detail and sitemap/SEO require approved ownership and approved shops. Product APIs use no-store responses. Media is served through the existing shops dispatcher route only after current shop approval, draft approval and attachment membership checks; bounded signature validation and nosniff apply. The binary media response avoids embedding megabytes of base64 in catalog DTOs. Previously downloaded public content cannot be revoked.

Cart additions, checkout quotes and order creation enforce server purchase eligibility. Order creation checks it inside the stock-reservation transaction, including the legacy COD path. Missing ownership, moderation removal and suspension fail closed. Unavailable items can still be removed from carts/wishlists. Client availability is informational, never authorization.

Gadgify is the customer payment merchant. After admin content approval and seller acceptance, existing Gadgify checkout may accept the item when checkout/payment settings are enabled. The fee amount is not added to the customer total: it is a shop-side fee snapshot. Coupon discounts are allocated deterministically to item lines; a percentage is calculated on that discounted item subtotal, excluding delivery and tax. Fixed fees apply per unit sold.

The prepared migration extends the order-item shop snapshot with the exact fee type/value/version, discounted fee base, calculated fee amount and status. Captured online orders and delivered COD orders accrue the fee as due to Gadgify; cancellation before payment voids a pending fee and a provider-confirmed full refund reverses a due fee. No fee is accrued from an unconfirmed payment. This is a durable charge calculation/record, not a seller payment collection, payout transfer or settlement reconciliation. Those operations and partial-refund fee adjustments remain pending.

## Owner decisions and rollout prerequisites

Per-product fee basis is approved as a fixed INR fee per unit sold or percentage of discounted item price; the admin chooses the actual amount/rate and the seller accepts each offer. Customer payments go to Gadgify through its existing payment setup. Delivery remains free under current owner direction and shops handle their own fulfillment. Automated fee collection from sellers, settlement/payouts, partial-refund allocation and financial/legal terms remain unresolved.

This code requires the prepared ownership and fulfillment migrations plus `20261002120000_marketplace_commercial_offers` and freshly generated Prisma types before rollout. **Deploying against an unmigrated database will make affected catalog/cart/checkout routes unavailable.** Rehearse backups/backfill, historical ownership and compatibility triggers first. The owner applies migrations and deploys; none were executed here. No new environment variable or provider setup is introduced by this change.

Tests authored for purchase eligibility, privacy, namespace separation, unavailable SEO offers and order rollback. Existing transaction fixtures now include platform ownership. API no-emit TypeScript configuration accepts explicit .ts imports so native Node regression imports resolve the shared policy. Test/lint/build/format execution remains deferred by the owner. Browser/mobile/auth isolation, Prisma generation, migration, Vercel media transport and production checks remain pending.
